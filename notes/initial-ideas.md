# Initial Ideas

- The proposal should avoid calling the system an ERP too early.
- The strongest anchor is operational control for trade-compliance dossiers.
- BOM is a gateway problem, not the full product definition.
- The client may eventually want a multi-client operations backbone, not just a Growatt-like case solution.
- The first release should probably win on document control, versioning, and traceability before advanced workflow automation.
- We should treat customs settlement and declaration work as first-class modules, not side notes under CO.
- A later proposal deck can reuse evidence from the Growatt case without dragging in raw case artifacts.
