# Solution Outline

## Working Product Definition

A platform to manage client dossiers, operational files, BOM versions, customs-linked data, and staff workflows for trade compliance work.

## Proposed Core Modules

## 1. Client Workspace

- client profile
- engagement status
- dossier grouping by shipment, declaration set, reporting period, or project
- assignment by staff and team

## 2. Document Control

- centralized upload and intake
- source channel tagging
- version history
- file relationships
- approval and superseded status

## 3. BOM and Material Control

- BOM versioning
- effective date ranges
- approval flow
- substitute-material rules
- traceability from exported product to BOM source

## 4. CO Operations

- dossier checklist
- RVC working data
- stock traceability
- supporting document linking
- export-package level status

## 5. Customs Operations

- import and export declaration working sets
- declaration-level document linking
- settlement-report preparation workspace
- reconciliation views across declarations, stock, and norms

## 6. Search and Audit

- find by client, product code, material code, declaration number, invoice, dossier
- who changed what and when
- reconstruct a past dossier state by date

## 7. Workflow and Assignment

- task ownership
- review queues
- handover support
- status transitions and blockers

## Suggested Delivery Phases

## Phase 1

- client workspace
- document control
- BOM and material control
- CO dossier workflow

## Phase 2

- declaration workspace
- customs settlement workspace
- traceability and reconciliation views

## Phase 3

- external integrations
- reporting layer
- controlled client portal access if needed

## What Should Stay Out Of Phase 1

- full accounting
- production planning
- full warehouse execution
- procurement execution
- broad ERP replacement claims
