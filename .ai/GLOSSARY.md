# Glossary

<!-- Format: **Term** — Definition -->
## BOM
Bill of materials or material norm used to define the components required for a finished product.

## CO
Certificate of origin dossier and the operational work around preparing, supporting, and explaining that dossier.

## BCQT
Customs settlement reporting work, including the reconciliation of imports, exports, norms, and stock.

## Trade-Compliance Operations Platform
Working term for the proposed system that manages client dossiers, documents, norms, declarations, and related operational data without claiming full ERP scope.

## Document Control
The capability to ingest, version, classify, relate, and trace files across dossiers and clients.
